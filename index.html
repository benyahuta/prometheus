Chapter 2 · Embeddings

Tokens become vectors

Next, each piece becomes a long list of numbers. This is how the model "knows" that cat and kitten mean similar things — their numbers point in similar directions. Related words literally sit near each other.

Chapter 3 · Attention

Words look at each other

Here's the problem: the word bat gets the exact same numbers whether you're talking baseball or caves. Attention is the fix — every word looks around at every other word, and context flows in to sharpen its meaning. Watch it switch targets when you change one word at the end.

Chapter 5 · The output

A bet on the next token

The model never "decides" on one answer. It bets on every possible next word at once, then rolls the dice. That's why regenerating gives you a different reply — and the temperature dial sets how daring the dice are.
