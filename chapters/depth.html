Residual streams & MLPs

Nothing replaces the vector — everything adds to it

a conveyor belt through the factory: every station adds a sticker; nobody repacks the box.

x ← x + Attention(x)     x ← x + MLP(x)

the straight line is the residual stream — each machine taps it, computes a correction, adds it back. a block with nothing to say adds ≈ 0, so 96-layer stacks still train. (LayerNorm re-standardizes before each tap — plumbing.)

Inside the MLP: expand, bend, contract

attention gathers the gossip; the MLP sits alone with each word and thinks it over.

MLP(x) = Wdown · GELU(Wup · x)   d → 4d → d

the bend is the whole point — without it, stacked matrices collapse into one matrix. attention routes, MLPs know: neurons here fire for things like “basketball words” or “inside a URL”.
