Queries, Keys & Values

“the robot ate apples”, computed for real — scroll, and the multiplications happen in front of you.

Q — query
“what am I looking for?”
K — key
“what do I contain?”
V — value
“what do I hand over if you attend to me?”
Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax QKT√dk V

① the input X

X  (4 tokens × 4 dims)
one row per token — dense and messy, like real embeddings

4 dims is for legibility — GPT-3's rows are 12,288 numbers long. same picture, ~3,000× wider.

② project: Q = X·WQ

WQ, WK, WV are the learned weights — training tunes these numbers; everything else on this page is pure arithmetic.

③ scores: every query × every key

no peeking at the future — masked cells become −∞

each cell of S is one dot product: a row of Q (what word i seeks) against a column of KT (what word j offers), shrunk by √dk. watch row ate spike at column robot.

④ softmax each row → attention weights

A = softmax(S)
each row sums to 100% · −∞ becomes exactly 0

⑤ output: a weighted mix of values

⑥ project back to the stream: O·WO

the head worked in 3 dims (dk); WO carries its verdict back to the full 4 dims of the residual stream — this is the correction that gets added to each token's vector. no dimension was harmed.

⑦ many heads, in parallel

MultiHead(X) = concat(head1, …, headh) · WO

each head has its own WQ, WK, WV — its own thing to hunt for. GPT-3 runs 96 of these per layer, at every layer, for every token, as one batched matrix multiply.
